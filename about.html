Introduction

Began as a joint venture with Milan.

We profoundly wish to introduce ourselves as Excel Craft Pvt. Ltd., formerly known as Redaelli (India) Pvt. Ltd., incorporated in the year 1994, a pioneer and leader in the field of manufacturing wire and cable making machines.

Our unit in India started as a joint venture with M/s Redaelli Techna Mechanica Group, Milano, Italy, who pioneered many developments in wire and cable machinery. During the joint venture, most of the technical know-how for manufacturing wire drawing machines for ferrous and non-ferrous material, drum twister lines, rigid stranding machines, LRPC lines, tubular stranding, skip stranding, bunching and twinning machines was made available to the Indian partner, M/s Redaelli (India) Pvt. Ltd., which changed its name to Excel Craft Pvt. Ltd. in 2005.

M/s Redaelli Techna Mechanica, Italy has been in the world market for over 80 years, with group companies M/s OTT, France (leaders in wire drawing machines) and M/s J.A. Kraft, Germany (pioneers in cable machinery). Their experience has given us a wide range of proven products and technology.

Today

Our continual developments in the design of mechanical and electrical features have proved to be at par with the needs of the latest global standards.

Excel Craft is a regular source of equipment for leading wire, rope and cable companies in India. We have also exported wire, rope and cable plants to Italy, Indonesia, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Jordan, and machines to Germany, the UAE, Kenya, Thailand and Nigeria.

The Patancheru industrial area contains numerous sub-vendors who support Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited as ancillaries, and whose shop facilities cover the manufacturing capability for the machine components of our entire product mix. Working with them is one of our core strengths: quality products with competitive deliveries and attractive economics.
