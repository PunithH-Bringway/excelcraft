Galvanizing & patenting

Furnace Take-Ups

Multi-head take-ups for galvanizing and patenting furnace lines, including accumulation-type spool take-ups and V-groove stationary take-up frames with pattern laying.

Reference installations

  • Polycab, Halol. 60-head take-ups for the galvanizing furnace of their armouring steel wire facility.
  • Tata Steel, Tarapur. 28-head accumulation-type 1120 mm spool take-ups for a patenting line.
  • Blue Nile Wire Products, Kenya. 16-head 650 mm V-groove stationary take-up frame with pattern laying, for a galvanizing furnace.

Galvanizing & patenting

Furnace Pay-Offs

Stationary single and dual pay-offs for multi-wire furnace lines, so that coils change without stopping the line.

Reference installations

  • Tata Steel, Tarapur. 28-head stationary dual-head pay-offs for a patenting line.
  • Blue Nile Wire Products, Kenya. 16-head stationary dual pay-offs for a galvanizing furnace.
  • Aarti Steels, Ludhiana. Pay-offs and take-ups for patenting and tyre bead plating lines.
