Privacy notice

Privacy & cookies.

How this website handles your information. In short: it does not collect any.

Cookies

This website does not set any cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising pixels and no social media trackers, and it does not build a profile of you.

Everything the site needs — fonts, images, stylesheets and documents — is served from this website itself, so your visit is not shared with any third party.

The only thing stored in your browser is a small note recording that you dismissed the privacy bar at the bottom of the page, so that it does not reappear on every visit. It stays on your device, holds no personal information, and clearing your browser data removes it.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form does not send anything to this website. When you press Send enquiry, your own email program opens with the message already written, addressed to our sales team. Nothing leaves your device until you press send in your email program, and we receive your enquiry as an ordinary email.

We use the details in that email only to answer your enquiry and to supply the machinery you ask about. We do not sell or rent them to anyone.

Server records

Like every website, the server that delivers these pages keeps standard technical records of requests, which can include your IP address, the time of the request and your browser type. They are used to keep the site running and secure.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to us and we will respond.
