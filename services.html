01

All types of wire drawing, wire rope and cable machinery

All these machines are vital equipment in the manufacturing sector for producing a wide array of wires, ropes and cables. Wire drawing machinery reduces wire rod diameter through successive die passes, producing wire of varying gauges. Wire rope machinery produces wire ropes for applications like construction and mining through stranding and twisting. Cable machinery manufactures cables for the electrical and communication sectors. Together, these machines ensure high-quality wires, ropes and cables, and the efficiency of the industries that rely on them.

02

Automation system for any special purpose machinery

Special purpose machines (SPM) and automation minimise the risk of human error and the fatigue of performing repetitive tasks. They ensure consistent quality and interchangeability of parts by following the same designed process each time, without shortcuts. SPMs and automatic machines are engineered to run continuously for 24 hours a day with minimal supervision, and are custom-designed and developed for each unique requirement.

03

Customised SCADA system

A customised SCADA (supervisory control and data acquisition) system is tailored to the unique requirements and processes of the client, offering enhanced control, monitoring and automation. Integrating sensors, controllers and data acquisition devices, it provides real-time data visualisation, analysis and decision-making support, with the flexibility, scalability and reliability to drive efficiency and productivity.

04

Centralised service support through telephone line and modem

Centralised service support enables efficient, convenient remote assistance: users reach technical support or troubleshooting by dialling a designated number or connecting through a modem. Whether resolving software issues, guiding equipment operation or offering maintenance assistance, it ensures prompt and effective problem-solving, with streamlined coordination and tracking of service requests.
