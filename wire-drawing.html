Dry drawing · SLM

High Speed Straight Line Wire Drawing Machines

A wide range of dry, straight-line drawing machines for low carbon, high carbon and stainless steel wire, used across steel wire, welding consumable and wire rope plants. Tuner-roller and full dancer-arm versions cover wire from 14 mm down to 0.6 mm, on drum sizes from 350 to 1200 mm, at up to 30 m/s.

Tungsten carbide coating on the blocks is available on request, and we manufacture rotating die boxes in-house to suit each customer's line.

Special features

  • No slip, no twist drawing. for high quality wire.
  • Limited dancer / tuner roller. highly responsive support for high-speed drawing.
  • Full dancer arm. controlled response for high-speed fine wire drawing.
  • AC inverter drives. low-inertia constant-HP motors for smooth running and low power consumption.
  • Heavy-duty European helical gearboxes. transmit heavy torque efficiently, with continuously lubricated bearings.
  • Induction-hardened forged alloy blocks. built for high wear resistance and maximum heat transfer.
  • Wide-gap turbulent water cooling. a special jacket creates turbulence for maximum heat transfer.
  • External air-blast cooling. additional air blast around the drum through an intricate sump.
  • Cassette die boxes. sturdy high-pressure design for better die cooling.
  • Brakes on the OTO and last blocks. for controlled stopping.
  • DIN 800 spooler, 1 tonne capacity. heavy-duty, for wire collection at 20 m/s.
  • In-house electronic programming. proven machine logic that accounts for every constraint of the drawing operation.

Overhead Take-Off (OTO) Wire Drawing Machines

OTO continuous drawing machines are valued for a simple structure, economy and easy operation. They draw low to high carbon wire, with a focus on mild steel, spring steel, welding wire and copper or aluminium wire, for wire sizes from 8 mm.

Fine wire · 11 to 23 dies

High Speed Wet Wire Drawing Machines

Wet drawing machines in 2, 4 and 6 spindle configurations, for fine wire from 3 mm inlet down to 0.17 mm. They produce CO2 welding wire, roping wire, spring wire, stainless steel wire and instrument wire, in high, medium and low carbon steel, black or galvanized.

Special features

  • Heavy-duty, robust, user-friendly construction
  • 2, 4 and 6 spindle designs, with single or double direction drawing
  • High-pressure jet cleaning of dies
  • Drawing cones in solid TC, TC-brazed or sprayed TC, with 3 to 7 cone steps per shaft
  • Water-cooled final capstan and a sensitive wire compensator
  • Designed for speeds up to 35 m/s
  • AC inverter electronics for better power efficiency
  • Inlet wire tensile range 40 to 140 kg/mm²
  • Main accessories: pay-off, OTO block, wire compensators, coiler block, spoolers from 150 to 630 mm
