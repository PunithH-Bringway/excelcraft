01About Excel Craft

Read our story

We’re Excel Craft Pvt. Ltd., previously known as Redaelli (India) Pvt. Ltd., established in 1994: proud pioneers in manufacturing wire and cable making machines.

Our journey began as a partnership with the Redaelli group of Milan, Italy, renowned for its innovations in wire and cable machinery. Through this collaboration we gained valuable technical expertise in manufacturing wire drawing machines, drum twisters, stranding machines and more, for both ferrous and non-ferrous materials. With over 30 years of experience, we are one of the leaders in the market.

04How a straight-line machine works

Draw the wire yourself.

Rod is pulled through a die before each capstan. Every pass makes it thinner and faster. Press and hold to run eight passes.

14.0Wire dia, mm
0.0Exit speed, m/s
0.0Area reduction, %

Hold the button, or hold Enter with it focused. Let go early and the line winds back down. The speeds follow volume constancy: a thinner wire must run faster.

  • No slip, no twist. Tuner-roller or full dancer-arm control between every block.
  • Cooled hard. Wide-gap turbulent water jackets plus air blast around every drum.
  • Built to last. Induction-hardened forged alloy blocks, tungsten carbide coating on request.
  • Programmed in-house. Proven drive logic from our own electrical team.
